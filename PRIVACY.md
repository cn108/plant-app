# Privacy Policy (draft)

_Last updated: replace with the publish date. Have this reviewed before a public release._

Plant Companion stores the following data:

- **Account data:** username, email address and a salted password hash (never the plain password).
- **Garden data:** the plants you add and their care history.
- **Community content:** forum posts and replies you submit, which other users can see.
- **Photos:** images you submit for identification are forwarded to the Pl@ntNet service and are not stored by this app's server.
- **Location:** the weather feature uses an approximate location to fetch local weather from Open-Meteo.

We do not sell personal data. You can ask for your account and data to be deleted by contacting the operator at the address you set here: `<contact email>`.
